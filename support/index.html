Support

Stairly for iPhone · Last updated 25 October 2026

Need help? Email support@lockvibe.app. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

Logging a climb

  1. Open the Today tab and tap Start climbing.
  2. Pick a course or start a free climb, then choose your equipment: stair machine or real stairs.
  3. When you finish, review the saved summary. Floors you logged are added to your vertical total, streaks, and landmarks.

Prefer Apple Health data? Stairly can read floors climbed recorded by your iPhone or watch and credit them to your session.

What is Pro?

Stairly is free to use without limits on logging. Pro adds the deeper layer:

  • All 12 guided courses, including stair-specific plans
  • Deep trends, personal bests, and per-mode breakdowns
  • Streak protection: two saved days per month
  • The full landmark collection above the first three
  • Watermark-free share cards and additional templates

Restore a purchase

Open Settings → Pro → Restore purchases, or tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the Pro screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by Stairly. Cancel or change a plan in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the current period ends.

Health permissions

If floors or workouts are not appearing, check iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Stairly, and confirm that Floors Climbed and Workouts are enabled for both reading and writing.

Notifications

Training reminders are local to your iPhone. If they do not arrive, check iOS Settings → Notifications → Stairly and make sure notifications are allowed.

Delete your data

All Stairly data lives on your device. Deleting the app removes workouts, achievements, and preferences. Workouts you saved to Apple Health remain in the Health app and can be removed there.
