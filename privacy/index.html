Privacy Policy

Stairly for iPhone · Last updated 25 October 2026

The short version: Stairly does not have accounts, servers, ads, or analytics. Your workouts are stored on your iPhone. We cannot see them.

1. Information we collect

We collect no personal information. Stairly has no sign-up, no login, no cloud sync, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not receive your name, email address, location, contacts, or any identifier that could be linked to you.

2. Information stored on your device

Stairly saves the following data locally on your iPhone so the app can work:

  • Workout records you create: floors, duration, pace, estimated vertical metres, calories, course, and timestamps
  • Progress data: achievements, streak history, and landmark progress
  • Preferences: training goal, equipment setup, reminder time, and display settings

This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you delete the app. Deleting the app does not remove workout data you chose to save to Apple Health (see below).

3. Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Stairly reads floors climbed and body mass from Apple Health, and writes workouts, floors climbed, and active energy back to Apple Health. Body mass is used only to estimate calories burned. Health data is accessed on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party. You can review or revoke these permissions at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

4. Notifications

Training reminders and streak notifications are scheduled locally by your iPhone. They are not sent through a server, and no notification content is shared with us.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Stairly Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and handles subscription management. We do not receive your payment details, billing address, or Apple Account information. Apple provides us only with aggregated sales reports that do not identify individual users.

6. Widgets

The Stairly widget reads a small summary of your own training data from a private container shared between the app and its widget extension on your device. This data is not transmitted anywhere.

7. Children

Stairly is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Your choices

  • Delete all app data by deleting Stairly from your iPhone.
  • Revoke Health access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Turn off notifications in iOS Settings → Notifications → Stairly.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
